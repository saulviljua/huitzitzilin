No one, nowhere, for anyone

I am no one, nowhere, for anyone. and yet I continue to pretend to be someone.

I never got into journaling as a habit, I've tried multiple times yet I can never figure out how to set my thoughts in paper or in words. I am often lost in my mind, thinking too much. I sometimes forget I live, but sometimes I wonder if that really is a bad thing. I'm often told I always seem to be thinking about something, and they're not wrong; I am. But, I don't think out of desire, I don't disassociate out of a desire to disassociate, but I just always end up back in my mind. There is a lot that I think about, too much that I dwell on and too much that gives me dread, a dread that looms over me 24/7. There are countless times I've desperately wanted to fall, times where I can't get up, where the idea of even continuing anything feels like an effort. And yet, for some reason, I still do. I still waltz into classes, I still smile to the people who talk to me, I still show up to the activities where no one but myself pushes me to go. Perhaps, stress is a cope for me. University has been difficult, of course, but the workload and academic pressure has never been the cause of my issues and torment. In fact, it is those pressures that relieves me of my internal struggles. Stress is my mask, what covers me and feeds me a false illusion of progress, shields me from what drags me down. Perhaps that is how I made it this far, perhaps that is why I made it to a top university. And yet, why must I still feel this way? How grateful do I have to be, to escape the poverty in which I had grown accustomed to, the poverty and violence of these broken streets, streets that I no longer need to abide by, and for some reason, I cannot help but call my home. And it is in this university where I am reminded that home is not the home I am supposed to be proud of, nor what I should consider a home at all. The streets I knew, a pride I carry, once again another wall that isolates me from the one thing I search for: love.

The love of God, is one that I continue to place my hope in. I believe in the one and true God, I believe in the Son and the Holy Spirit. He is my savior, the one and only hope I have left. I know if my faith ever disappears, I too will leave with it. However, despite my struggles with faith, the battle that I have faced for years against the devil, my faith is pure and strong. Love, God's love, is what keeps me going. But I crave human love, a love that God calls us to search for. But for me, it is not that I cannot find love, but I cannot accept it. And this is a truth that I have only just realized, and it pains me to know that my own pride and barriers are the root of all evil in me. I am no Saint, far from it, I am a sinner who has worked against others. I have put others down, I have used others for my own benefit, I do not deserve love. And even if that era of sin is in the past, a fruit of the world that I was brought into, I still hold a sinful pride that I cannot shake off of me. The white man is the enemy, and despite my calling to love the enemy, I know deep down, I am only but human. A human who is descendant of the original sin. Love is something that I am not new too, before I forgot to love, I loved someone for some time, before my ego got the best of me. Now, after all those years, I love again, a true and pure love, purer than the one I held before, a love gifted from the Holy Spirit. And my voyage through university has been the fight for this love, the love that I seek is great, yet challenging. A love tied to faith, it is what keeps me going. I do not know where I am going, and I do not know how to get there, but I hope eventually, God will finally grant me the one thing that He promised me, and that is love. The kind of love that holds no lust. The kind of love that holds no pride. The kind of love that proceeds from the Father and the Son, the kind of love that defines the Holy Spirit. And despite my mortal self, I will be there, as a follower of the Lord.

I am no one, nowhere, but through faith I can be free.
