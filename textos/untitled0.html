November 18th, 2025

Untitled

Returning back to the US I was immediately placed into the new world that was Notre Dame. For years isolated in the streets of Westside and in certain connections in high school, it was a difficult transition. I fell into a deep depression. Without knowing, I grew resentment and created a deep self-isolation. Since then I set myself in constant self reflection, and the journey has proven to be a difficult internal battle. Constantly I questioned my purpose outside the ghetto. I was defined by my wrongs, defined by the trauma and turmoil that is my past. I believed that I was not worthy of love, and as a result resentment shifted inward. I found myself isolating not the outside, but the outside from myself. However, through this self-reflection I learnt to open up slowly but gradually, to reopen pieces of faith that I had long forgotten. As a result, for the first time I have found myself released from a great guilt. But I am still far from healing. I continue to fear myself and fear others, I fear hurting those I love and being hurt myself. But no longer does noise weigh my mind, no longer does guilt keep my head down. I can walk outside and I realize I no longer hear it, and I can sit down and notice it is quiet. I have learnt a lot up to now; to live with my imperfections, and to accept the person I am for who I am. I realize that I don’t need to reject love, I don’t need to reject myself and define myself with the guilt and trauma that I was accustomed to guiding me. I don’t need to save others from myself, and I don’t need to be saved from others. I’m often sensitive, often in thought and lost in my own broken world, but I now believe I am not undeserving of the life or light that I seek. Therefore, I set myself free from guilt. I set myself free from the past. I set myself free from those I hurt and those I learnt to forgive. I set myself free from myself. My revival with faith has been my ultimate guide, and I can truly say that I am no longer lost. I trust that I can find what I need, whatever it may be, and I know I can find it eventually. I step into my own path. Not through you, not through them, but through me and the Above.
