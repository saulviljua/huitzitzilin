October 28th, 2025

Perspective tranquility

I am left here to rot.

An individual without feel, an individual who can't feel right. I feel all that I could possibly feel, for all and by all. I feel for myself, and often I am reminded that what I feel isn't feel. I feel resent, I feel faith, I feel desperation. I feel love. I love too much, and I never receive love. I can't be loved, I cannot accept love, I will never be loved. But I realize it's my own fault. I realize I cannot love because deep down, I have a deep resentment for all that is love. I love so much I hate, I hate so much I despise, I despise so much I become hate. I am the antithesis of love. I am nothing but a misguided love, a love that is built on foundations of guilt and passion. A passion of fire. A passion of thorns. I am no friend, I am no disciple. I am nothing but a faith driven contradiction, a man who I know despises love, a man who can never admit that deep down never deserved love. I am no one, and I will be no one. Because I am what I was always built to be. A man built from hate. A man built from the generation guilt that haunts the real world; A contradiction of holy nature. Made to despise those who can be loved because I cannot be loved. I despise love because I cannot know love. I am left here to be an example of the fire that burns all. I am the enemy. I am the one who will be vanquished, I am the one who will be slayed by the sword of the holy. I am embraced by no one but unwillingly my own flesh and body, I am bound by my ego and pride, by the guilt that corrodes my being, starved of the love that I can never reach. I am a broken individual, I am far from grace. I am thrown from the gates; I observe behind them. I am distant and infinitely away from peace. I put people down not out of conscious, but out of unavoidable sin. I reject love not out of passion, but out of imprinted guilt. And perhaps this is my grace for the world. I cannot be the light, I cannot be the love that is needed. I cannot be there for those I love, I cannot be who I desire to be. I cannot be life, I must always be inferior to the living. Because I do not live, I cannot learn the ability to live. Without life I cannot feel, and perhaps that's the truth of it all. All that I feel is an illusion, all that wells inside is nothing of value. All that I see is a channel through which I may view the world of God and His love bathe in hope, but never to be able to touch. So I stand here unable to love. This is the reality in front of my eyes. I rot here knowing I cannot be loved. Perhaps that is all I will ever be. Left to be nothing, watching those who can be something receive the blessings and love I never deserved. Either way there is no point to my ramblings. I continue my sense of "is" behind the river. I cannot cross it, and I can do nothing but remain here. My peace.
