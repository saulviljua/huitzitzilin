Hope and death

I think a lot about death. Funny enough, often throughout my life, death has always been on my mind. Through fear, through curiosity, or through hopelessness, death tends to be the one thing that the mind turns to. It isn't surprising, death is the ultimate finale to our lives, the goal of all of humanity, whether we like it or not, it is our ultimate finish line, the moment when we can finally escape the physical world.

I cannot deny that I have in the past, weighted the greater pain between life or a premature death.

Of course, we cannot die on our own terms. We cannot take our own lives, it is an egotistic action in nature, and goes against the teachings of Jesus. Rejecting life is rejecting God. But, life is not easy, and often times I recall walking through the forest behind my university, late at night or during the sunset hours, wondering and pondering how much better life really is compared to death. Faith is my only hope that I have left, to be honest. Without faith, I genuinely have nothing left. Without faith, I have no reason, no purpose, no hope to pursue. Perhaps that is why I have grown so religious these past few years, in that I no longer have a reason derived from the physical world to live, and out of desperation I attempt to find it in the spiritual. I love my religion, however, and I will continue to love God and my savior. So many nights I recall, disappearing into those forests, so many times where I have just walked around aimlessly, wishing to cry, yet for some reason I cannot. Often times, during these dark moments, my phone ridicules me with images and conversations of friends laughing and smiling, spending time together without pain in their hearts. It becomes frustrating, and a hatred forms. It is a cycle, in a sense. A cycle where isolation breads solitude, and that solitude breads hate, further building my walls higher and casting me in greater self isolation. It is a curse, it is a way of life I cannot break free from. Many times I've questioned my faith, why is it that I continue to believe that things will get better? Why is it that I continue to trust that there is hope for me, that there is love waiting for me when I simply cannot see it, and cannot feel it? Hell is... a scary place. Not in the sense of fire and torture, but in a sense of no hope. A sense that, hell is a place where the light of God cannot be found, and once one arrives in hell, one truly does no longer have any hope to hold on to. If my faith dies, the Earth will be my hell. At that point, it is a decision between the uncertainty of death, and the certainty of hell through life. Without faith, I have no hope, and without hope, I have no reason to live.

I do not plan on a premature death. My faith is strong, and despite all the challenges, all of the moments of doubt that the devil blinds me with, I trust that there is hope through God. I trust that there is hope out there for me, and that there is hope for me to find love, to find grace and to find peace in life and in death. However it is so difficult for me to find these things, and often it feels so impossible to find these graces, and yet time after time, God assures me that He loves me, and grants me a peace that I cannot quite describe. I recall every time in the chapel or at the alter I tend to go to, I am moved with a deep peace, a peace that surrounds me and grants me a moment of solace that I cannot explain. There is no reason for me not to believe, not to trust the faith that I hold, on the contrary, everything seems to work with faith. It's almost funny how much God is able to assure me everything will be okay even in my darkest moments, even when I simply cannot find a single way out of the dark. I still grieve, I still fall into despair, but I trust the Lord, I really do, and I really hope that in the coming years, I can finally find the fruit of everything that I tolerate. All the pain and the hurt, I trust one of these days it will all be carried off by the Holy Spirit. I trust I'll find the love, the pure love of the Holy Spirit, that I am searching for. And I trust one day I'll be loved on this Earth too.
