Rejection of love

Recently, I have sinned once more. Despite having gone a while without sinning, I fell into the blinding of the devil, and once again, I stand here, knowing that I have failed in the eyes of the Lord. Perhaps it is because I am away from my Catholic institution for fall break, and the reminder of the Lord's proximity is far away. It shouldn't be an excuse, however. I shouldn't forget that the Holy Spirit guides me even if the physical reminders and institutions that deliberately aid my faith are far away. And yet, still... I am here, once again at the mercy of the devil, as a sinner. I have been here before, I have been at this low before, feeling the grief and humiliation that prevents me from even looking at God, makes me feel unworthy of prayer, of forgiveness, of salvation entirely. Of course, we all know that God loves us despite sin, but still, I cannot help but feel an immense guilt when it comes to sin. I am no pure soul, I know. My past is one of sin and toxicity, the streets that made me cast in dark, no matter the good people that existed in them, we were all a part of a community that didn't understand peace. Perhaps that is why, during this fall break back in these streets, it is so easy to fall, as the devil holds his grip in our starvation for hope.

I am someone who is desperate for love. Not in the sense of non-platonic love or in the sense of lust, but pure love, love that proceeds from the Father and the Son: the Holy Spirit. The love that is unconditional, the love that one feels for a close friend, or for a family member. The love between two people united in the sacred act of marriage, the beauty that is the union of two people under God. Love, the love that Jesus holds for all of us, even during the crucifixion. I believe that we all desire this love, behind our pride, behind our lust, what we desire is the true love that God already holds for each and every one of us. And despite that, I cannot feel it. Constantly I am alone, constantly I have no one to turn to. Of course, I have my friends, I have people who care for me, who talk to me, but yet I still hold no best friend, no pure friend, no one that I can truly consider someone who I can trust with my life. I do not desire lust, I do not desire empty relationships, I merely desire love, love for me-Is that ego? I realize that, despite my constant crave for love, I often reject that very love. It's a dangerous form of rejection, one that hides behind the illusion of disinterest, of humiliation, of a sense of worthlessness. It isn't until literally a few days ago, right here in my broken streets, that I realize that the love I search for, the love that I cannot find and frustrates me, is a love that can come so easily to people. Why is that? I realize that love cannot be given if one does not accept it. And subconsciously, I realize that the devil has strung me to reject all love. No one hates me, no one despises me, no one considers me an obstacle, unworthy of love, except me. Of course, the world is broken, a world built from sin... but I realize that I too forget that I am built from sin, and my own sinful nature is what denies the one thing that I have searched for: love.

Sitting here, once again reminded of my recent sin, I realize that this concept of rejection is displayed with my relationship with God as well, not just with other people. We are often told that God loves us despite sin, that God, no matter who we are, no matter our sinful nature, loves us and cares for us. But despite that, I reject that love, when I sin I feel unworthy of His love, unworthy of prayer, unworthy of being His son. Is that not precisely what the devil desires from us? Often times, I realize, we desire love, we search for it, but we forget that to find love we must accept it, accept our worthiness of love, accept the unconditional love that comes from the Holy Spirit. We forget that subconsciously or consciously, we reject love. And as for me, I too have rejected love, and unfortunate as it may be, I believe this is the reason for the solitude and isolation that I have constantly suffered. I am a man of a broken mentality, a man that struggles to understand his own guilt and trauma. However, my one and only desire, the hope that God will grant me the beauty of love, keeps me going. So, indeed I am a sinner, one who sins and will continue to sin. But despite all of that, be reminded that we are not unworthy of love, no matter how much we may feel like we aren't. God loves you, and I promise that there is someone out there that loves you too. Accept that love.
